Before giving any helper a spec, brief the owner on its decisions and get agreement. On 8 October 2026 the S27 letterhead/catalogue spec went to the helper without discussion and the owner called it out. UI fixes the owner raises come first; helper lanes run alongside only once their spec is agreed.

**Why:** the owner decides product scope; a helper building an undiscussed spec wastes a lane and bypasses them.
**How to apply:** draft the spec, summarise its key decisions to the owner (AskUserQuestion for the open ones), hand over only after agreement. Related: [[discuss-before-each-session]], [[codex-briefs-fully-decided]].
