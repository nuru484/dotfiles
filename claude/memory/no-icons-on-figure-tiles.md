Never add icons to figure tiles, stat cards or the cards inside a section
(FigureTiles, ageing buckets, report overview figures), anywhere in LFMS.

**Why:** owner, 6 October 2026, after seeing tinted icon discs on the report
overview tiles: "the icons with the cards doesn't work, it looks terrible,
so remove it and never make it like that anywhere."

**How to apply:** when a screen looks flat, change layout (side by side,
width to content) rather than decorating figures. Also: when re-laying out a
page, move only what was asked; don't pull sections out of their cards or
reorder them (same day, the ageing tiles were wrongly moved up and
uncarded). Related: [[no-shadows-unless-explicit]].
